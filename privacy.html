Privacy Policy — Poker Cascade

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains which data the Poker Cascade game (Android app) uses, why, and what your rights are. It applies to the app published on Google Play.

Who is responsible

Poker Cascade is published under the developer name Jaci Games. The person responsible for the data is Miguel Marinho Bencardino, in Brazil.

For privacy questions, write to jaci.gamesstudio@gmail.com.

In short

  • No sign-up, name, e-mail or password is needed to play.
  • Your progress stays on your phone only: levels, stars, chips, decks and settings. We do not send this data to any server of ours.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. For that, Google collects some device data, explained below.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional. You choose whether to watch them.
  • There are no in-app purchases in this version.

Data the game keeps on your device

The game saves your progress and preferences on the phone itself:

  • levels won and stars;
  • chips and their history;
  • decks, powers and achievements;
  • volume, language and helps turned on.

This data never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in the Android settings, deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob, Google's ad service, to show rewarded videos (for example "watch a video to double your chips" or "+3 moves").

To choose and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers: Android advertising ID and app set IDAdvertising, measurement and fraud prevention
Ad interactions: app launches, taps, videos watchedAdvertising and measurement
Diagnostics: crashes and performanceMeasurement and fraud prevention
IP address, which may indicate an approximate location (city or region, never the exact address)Advertising and fraud prevention

How Google handles this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Content limit: we ask Google only for ads suitable for a general audience. Gambling and betting categories are blocked.

How to control ads

  • Advertising ID: on Android, under Settings → Google → Ads (the path may vary), you can delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Europe and the United Kingdom: the first time, the game asks for your consent to personalized ads, through Google's consent platform. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy, inside the game.
  • Without personalized ads the game works normally. The ads shown are just less related to your interests.

Data collected by Google Play

Google Play may report to us, in aggregate and without identifying you, figures such as installs, ratings and crash reports. This follows the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Poker Cascade is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child used the app and data about them was collected, write to jaci.gamesstudio@gmail.com.

Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018), and similar laws where you live, you may ask for:

  • confirmation that we process your data, and access to it;
  • correction, anonymization or deletion;
  • information on who we share it with;
  • withdrawal of consent.

Since we keep no data about you on servers of ours, most of these requests are solved by clearing the app's data on the phone or adjusting the ad options. Advertising data is handled by Google, and you can also exercise your rights directly with Google.

For any request, write to jaci.gamesstudio@gmail.com. We answer within 15 days.

Security

Data sent by the ads SDK travels encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If the game adds purchases, an online mode or another service that uses data, this policy will be updated first. The date at the top shows the latest change.
